---
name: Storefront home routing
description: Product navigation expectation confirmed by the user
---

The storefront homepage should remain the destination for the logo and Home navigation even when a visitor is signed in; account/profile pages are separate destinations.

**Why:** The user reported that redirecting signed-in visitors from Home to their profile was wrong and confirmed restoring the storefront homepage worked.

**How to apply:** Preserve this expectation when changing authentication redirects or shared navigation.
